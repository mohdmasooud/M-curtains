How to Measure Your Windows Perfectly

Follow our 4-step artisan standard to ensure a bespoke fit with 0mm error margin.

Curtain Track Width

Measure the total end-to-end length of the track rail. If your track has an overlap in the middle, measure the full rail length plus the overlap distance.

Curtain Pole Width

Measure the pole length between the finials (decorative end plugs). Never include the decorative finials in your width measurement.

No Track Fitted Yet?

Measure the window frame width, then add 15 to 25 cm on each side (30-50 cm total). This allows curtains to draw fully off the window glass.

From Curtain Tracks

Measure from the very top of the track rail down to your desired bottom hem position (1.5cm above floor for floating, or touching floor).

From Pole with Rings

Measure from the small eyelet loop at the bottom of the curtain ring (where the hook inserts) down to the floor or sill.

For Eyelet / Grommets

Measure from the top of the pole down to the floor, then we automatically add 3.5cm header stand-up above the grommet hole.

Master Rules to Follow:
  • Always measure with a metal tape measure (cloth tapes stretch over time and cause 2-3 cm errors).
  • Measure in 3 places (left, center, right). Floors and ceilings are rarely perfectly level! Always use the shortest drop for floating hems.
  • Check for radiator and sill projections so curtains fall cleanly without catching on handles.
Common Mistakes to Avoid:
  • Mounting curtain rods right against the window top (mount 15-20cm above to double perceived room height!).
  • Using only 1 panel for a wide window (stack-back becomes too bulky on one side).
  • Forgetting that velvet relaxes and stretches 0.5-1 cm under its own weight after hanging.
