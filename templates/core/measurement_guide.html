Professional Hanging Styles

1. Hover / Floating (1-2 cm above floor)

Clean and contemporary. Avoids dust accumulation and lets curtains swing freely without touching floorboards.

2. Gentle Breaking / Kiss (Touches floor)

Add 1-2 cm to your measurement. The hem fabric breaks gently upon the floor for a tailored, elegant look.

3. Dramatic Puddle (5-10 cm pooling)

Best suited for heavyweight velvet and pure linen drapes. Creates a romantic, opulent chateau ambiance.

Designer Pro Tip:

Mount your curtain rod 15–20 cm above the window frame and extend 15–25 cm past each side. This visually doubles ceiling height and allows maximum daylight into the room!
