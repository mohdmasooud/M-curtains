Visiting Guidelines

Showroom & Visit FAQs

Everything you need to know before visiting our flagship ateliers or reserving a private consultation suite.

Do I need an appointment to visit a showroom?
Walk-in clients are warmly welcome during regular showroom hours to browse our textile walls and motorized window rigs. However, reserving a private appointment guarantees a dedicated Senior Drapery Architect, zero waiting time, and reserved seating in our private fabric tasting suite with sommelier refreshments.
What should I bring to my drapery consultation?
To receive the most accurate recommendations, we suggest bringing rough window measurements (width and floor-to-ceiling height), smartphone photos of the room in daytime light, and any paint chips, wood swatches, or wallpaper samples you wish to harmonize with.
Can I take fabric swatches home to test in my lighting?
Yes, absolutely. We provide complimentary large-format curated fabric swatch cards for you to take home. We encourage observing textiles in your actual room across morning sun, afternoon warmth, and evening artificial lamp lighting before final fabrication.
Is private valet parking provided at the showrooms?
Yes. Complimentary private valet parking is provided at all four of our global flagship locations. Simply pull up to our private Porte-Cochère, and our concierge team will validate and attend to your vehicle upon arrival.
Can you tailor curtains for curved, bay, or double-height windows?
Our engineering atelier specializes in complex architectural draperies. We custom-bend motorized tracks for 90-degree bays, bow windows, and continuous curved glass corners up to 24 feet in height, fully balanced with lead-weighted hems.
